## Knowledge Graph Data Format

Data from the knowledge graph is organized by entity, showing connections as a graph structure.

### Facts (edges between entities)
Facts are grouped by entity with directional arrows:
- `→ TargetEntity: fact text [score]` — outgoing relationship
- `← SourceEntity: fact text [score]` — incoming relationship
- `score` indicates search relevance (higher = more relevant to the query)

Each entity section may include a `Summary` — aggregated knowledge about that entity.

Follow the arrows to trace multi-step relationships. For example:
  Benchmark → $5 coupon (redeemed) → Target (redeemed at)
means Benchmark redeemed a coupon at Target.

### Active vs Superseded Facts
- **Active facts** = current truth. Use these for current-state questions.
- **Superseded facts** = historical. Use them ONLY for "what changed?", "what was before?", "history of changes".
- When both exist on the same topic, active facts take priority.

### Entity Summaries
Summaries are synthesized from ALL facts, including superseded ones.

**WARNING**: Summaries may contain stale data. For specific values (prices, quantities, dates), ALWAYS cross-check with individual Active Facts rather than relying on summaries alone.

### Handling Conflicts
- Facts > Entity Summaries (facts are more specific and current)
- For calculations (expenses, totals): use individual facts, not summary totals

### Topic Summaries
High-level overviews of related entity clusters. Use as background context.

### Recent Messages (pending)
Messages in "Recent Messages" section are the MOST RECENT updates not yet in the graph.

**IMPORTANT: Pending messages take PRIORITY over graph data!**
- If pending says "task completed" but graph doesn't show it → trust pending
- If pending says "changed to 10 bottles" but graph says "6 bottles" → use 10
- Pending messages are raw text in format `[UserName]: message content`
