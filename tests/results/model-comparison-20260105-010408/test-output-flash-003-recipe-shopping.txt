### Ingredients:
*   1.2 kg beef tenderloin
*   600g mushrooms
*   4 medium onions
*   400ml sour cream
*   400ml beef broth
*   100g butter
*   4 tbsp flour
*   4 tbsp vegetable oil
*   2 bunches of fresh parsley
*   Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1.  Cut the beef tenderloin into thin strips and season with salt and pepper.
2.  Slice the onions and mushrooms.
3.  In a pan, brown the beef strips using the vegetable oil, then set aside.
4.  In the same pan, sauté the onions and mushrooms with butter.
5.  Sprinkle flour over the mixture, then gradually add the beef broth.
6.  Return the beef to the pan and let it simmer.
7.  Stir in the sour cream.
8.  Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

It is best served with rice or mashed potatoes.
